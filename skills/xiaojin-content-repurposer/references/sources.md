# Sources and Adaptation Notes

This skill is an independently maintained adaptation for multi-platform long-form content workflows.

- The source-analysis, platform-restructuring, and self-review sequence was informed by [qianjin-content-repurposer](https://github.com/ZOORO-NEW/qianjin-content-repurposer). Its MIT license is retained in LICENSE-qianjin.
- Source-gap labeling and cross-platform fact tracking were informed by [platform-content](https://github.com/arm-pz/platform-content). Its MIT license is retained in LICENSE-platform-content.
- The skill's workflow, examples, and checklists are adapted for X Articles, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat public accounts, and Feishu tutorials.
- Unsupported growth claims, fixed audience profiles, and unverified platform-algorithm claims are intentionally excluded.
- No credentials, account data, private source drafts, or local knowledge-base paths are included.
